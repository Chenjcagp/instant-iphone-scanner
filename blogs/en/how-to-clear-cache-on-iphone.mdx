Nothing is more annoying than trying to take a photo and seeing the **"Storage Full"** pop-up.

You've already deleted your old photos. You've cleared your messages. But your iPhone still says it's full. The culprit is often **hidden cache** and **"System Data"**.

In this guide, we'll show you 3 proven ways to clear cache on iPhone and reclaim gigabytes of space.

## Method 1: Clear Safari Cache (The Biggest Culprit)

Every website you visit saves data (images, scripts, cookies) to your phone. Over time, this can grow to several gigabytes.

**How to clear it:**
1.  Open **Settings**.
2.  Scroll down and tap **Safari**.
3.  Scroll to the bottom and tap **Clear History and Website Data**.
4.  Confirm by tapping **Clear History**.

*Note: This will log you out of websites, so make sure you know your passwords.*

## Method 2: Offload Unused Apps

We all have apps we downloaded once and never used again. These apps hoard storage space.

**How to automate this:**
1.  Open **Settings**.
2.  Tap **App Store**.
3.  Toggle on **Offload Unused Apps**.

This feature automatically deletes the app binary but keeps your documents and data. It’s a lifesaver for 64GB or 128GB iPhones.
