You have a contract or a form that needs to be signed immediately. You are holding your iPhone.

Normally, you would go to the App Store, download a "Scanner App," scan your document, and then realize they want **$9.99/month** just to let you add a signature.

**Stop. You don't need to pay for that.**

In this guide, I will show you a **100% free workflow** to scan and sign any document on your iPhone using tools you already have: **Safari** and **iOS Markup**.

## Step 1: Scan the Document (Privacy-First)

First, we need to turn that paper into a digital PDF.
We use **Instant iPhone Scanner** because it runs in your browser and processes images locally (so your sensitive contracts are never uploaded to a server).

1.  Open **[Instant iPhone Scanner](https://scanoniphone.com)** in Safari.
2.  Tap **"Take Photo"** and capture your document.
3.  The tool will auto-crop and enhance the text.
4.  Tap **"Download PDF"** and choose **"Save to Files"**.

## Step 2: Open the Built-in Signing Tool

You don't need a third-party app to sign. iPhone has a powerful feature called **Markup** hidden inside the Files app.

1.  Go to your home screen and open the **Files** app (the blue folder icon).
2.  Find the PDF you just downloaded.
3.  Tap to open it.
4.  Look for the **Pen Icon** (Markup) in the bottom-right or top-right corner.

## Step 3: Add Your Signature

Here is the magic trick:

1.  Tap the **"+" (Plus)** icon in the Markup toolbar (usually at the bottom right).
2.  Select **"Signature"** from the menu.
3.  If you haven't used it before, use your finger to draw your signature on the screen.
4.  Tap **"Done"**.

Your signature will now appear as a floating sticker on the document!

![How to find the signature tool in iPhone Markup menu](/images/blog/sign-documents-on-iphone-ipad.jpg)

## Step 4: Position and Save

1.  Drag the signature to the correct line on the document.
2.  Drag the blue corner dots to resize it so it fits perfectly.
3.  Tap **"Done"** in the top-left corner to save the PDF.

## Summary

You just saved $10/month and kept your data private.

* **Scanning**: Powered by [Instant iPhone Scanner](https://scanoniphone.com) (Web-based, Free).
* **Signing**: Powered by iOS Files (Built-in).

**Need to sign legally binding contracts?**
If you are signing high-stakes business agreements that require an audit trail, this free method might not be enough. For those cases, we recommend using professional tools like **DocuSign** or **Adobe Sign**. But for everyday receipts, permission slips, and simple forms, the method above is perfect.
