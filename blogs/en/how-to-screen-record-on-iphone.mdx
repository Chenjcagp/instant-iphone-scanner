You want to show a friend how to use an app, or capture a funny bug in a game. The easiest way is to use the iPhone's built-in **Screen Recording** feature.

But out of the box, the recording button might be missing from your Control Center. And often, people record videos with **no sound**.

In this guide, we'll fix all of that.

## Step 1: Add the Record Button

If you don't see the circle icon in your Control Center, you need to enable it first.

1.  Open **Settings** > **Control Center**.
2.  Scroll down to find **Screen Recording**.
3.  Tap the green **(+)** button next to it.

Now, swipe down from the top-right corner of your screen. You should see the record button (it looks like a solid circle inside a ring).

![iPhone Control Center Screen Recording Button](/images/blog/screen-record-step1.jpg)

## Step 2: How to Record with Sound (Microphone)

This is the most common mistake. By default, iPhone **only records system sounds** (the app's music), but **NOT your voice**.

**To record your voice commentary:**
1.  Open Control Center.
2.  **Long press** (hold down) the Screen Recording button.
3.  Tap the **Microphone** icon at the bottom to turn it **ON** (it will turn red).
4.  Tap **Start Recording**.

## Step 3: Stop Recording

To stop, simply tap the **red clock** in the top-left corner of your screen, then tap **Stop**. The video will be saved to your Photos app.

![How to stop screen recording on iPhone](/images/blog/screen-record-step2.jpg)

## Pro Tip: When NOT to Screen Record

Screen recording is perfect for games and videos, but it is **terrible for saving text or documents**.

Why? Because video files are **huge**. A 1-minute video can take up **100MB+** of space. If you use video to record a receipt, a letter, or homework, you are wasting your iPhone's storage.

**The Better Alternative:**
If you need to capture **physical documents or paper**, use a scanner instead of a camera video.

Try **[Instant iPhone Scanner](https://scanoniphone.com)** directly in your browser.
* **It creates PDFs**, not heavy videos.
* **It saves space:** A PDF is usually only 0.2MB (vs 100MB for video).
* **It's clearer:** Much easier to read than a shaky video.

Use the right tool for the job: Screen Record for **motion**, Scan for **information**.
