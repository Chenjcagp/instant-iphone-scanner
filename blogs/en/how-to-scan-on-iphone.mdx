## Method 2: Using the Apple Notes App

If you prefer using Apple's built-in tools and don't mind a few extra taps, the **Notes app** has a powerful scanner hidden inside it. This is great if you want to save the scan into your iCloud notes for later.

### Step-by-Step Guide:

1.  Open the **Notes** app on your iPhone.
2.  Create a **New Note** (tap the icon in the bottom right corner).

3.  Tap the **Camera Icon** 📷 in the toolbar above the keyboard.
4.  Select **"Scan Documents"** from the pop-up menu.
5.  Point your camera at the document. It will try to auto-capture.
6.  Tap **"Save"** to finish.

**The Downside:** The scanned PDF is now trapped inside a Note. To share it, you have to open the note, tap the PDF, tap the Share icon, and then choose where to send it.

## Method 3: Using the Files App

Did you know the **Files** app can also scan? This is the best method if you want to save the PDF directly to a specific folder in your iCloud Drive or On My iPhone.

### How to do it:

1.  Open the **Files** app.
2.  Go to the folder where you want to save the scan (e.g., "Downloads").

There is no standalone "Scanner" app icon. Apple hides the scanner feature inside the **Notes** app, the **Files** app, and sometimes the **Mail** app. This is why many users prefer bookmarking a web scanner for easier access.
