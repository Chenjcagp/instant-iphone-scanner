## The Story Behind Instant Scanner

We built **Instant iPhone Scanner** out of frustration.

Like you, we often need to scan a document quickly—a receipt, a contract, or a handwritten note. But whenever we searched for a "scanner app" on the App Store, we ran into the same problems:

* ❌ **Bloated Apps:** Why do I need to download 200MB just to take a photo?
* ❌ **Forced Sign-ups:** I just want a PDF, not another account.
* ❌ **Subscription Traps:** "Free trial" buttons that turn into $50/year subscriptions if you forget to cancel.
* ❌ **Privacy Concerns:** Why do my personal documents need to be uploaded to a cloud server?

### We believed there had to be a better way.

So we built it.

**Instant iPhone Scanner** is a web-based tool that uses the advanced camera capabilities already built into your iPhone's browser.

### Our Promise to You

* **100% Local Privacy:** The scanning process happens directly in your browser using WebAssembly technology. Your documents **never** leave your device. We don't see them, and we can't save them.
* **Always Free:** No watermarks. No limits. No "Pro Version" needed for basic tasks.
* **Zero Friction:** No app store. No download. No install. Just open the link and scan.

We hope this tool saves you time and keeps your digital life a little bit simpler.
