## Privacy Policy for Instant iPhone Scanner

**Effective Date:** January 26, 2026

At **Instant iPhone Scanner**, we prioritize your privacy above all else. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle your data when you use our website.

### 1. No Data Collection (Local Processing)
The core feature of our service is **client-side processing**.
* When you select or take a photo, it is processed **entirely within your web browser**.
* Your images are **NEVER uploaded** to our servers or any third-party cloud storage.
* We do not have access to, nor do we store, any of the documents you scan.

### 2. Analytics
We may use anonymous analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand how users interact with our site (e.g., page views, session duration). This data is aggregated and **does not contain any personal information** or content from your documents.

### 3. Cookies
We use minimal cookies strictly necessary for the website's operation (e.g., remembering your language preference). We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes without your explicit consent.

### 4. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes.

### 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository.
